Title: How I’d Use ChatGPT for SEO in 2026

Source: https://youtu.be/zb5oTzHd914?si=_TdcY8YtRsQDoiwy

Transcript:

I’m going to show you 10 proven ways to use ChatGPT for SEO without spamming or doing anything risky. These techniques will save you a massive amount of time and effort in your SEO campaigns.

My name is Nathan Gotch. I’ve led hundreds of successful SEO campaigns over the last decade, and I’m the CEO of Rankability, a top AI SEO software company.

1. Filter keywords by relevance

Start with a huge list of keywords from a keyword research tool. In this example, I’m using Rankability to download the keyword list I want to filter.

The goal is to take a large list, maybe 400 keywords, and narrow it down to the most relevant ones to pursue. This is the first stage of filtering, and it is the most important.

You want to understand how close each keyword is to making money for the business. The shorter that gap, the better the keyword. The longer the gap, the lower the priority.

For example, if the business is a bankruptcy lawyer in Atlanta, you paste in the keyword list and let ChatGPT assign a relevance score.

2. Group keywords into clusters

Once you have keyword relevance, group the good keywords into buckets and clusters.

In SEO, you want to focus on one cluster at a time. You do not want to spread yourself across unrelated topics. Go deep on one cluster, then move to the next.

This helps build real topic authority on your website. After that, you can take the same topics and build content around them on YouTube, LinkedIn, and other platforms.

3. Create new content that ranks

You can use ChatGPT to create content for commercial pages.

In this example, the process starts by crawling a specific business website, pulling out unique selling propositions and testimonials, and turning that into a prompt for Rankability’s AI writer.

You also include the primary keyword and the intent of the page, such as commercial intent.

The key is to use the thinking model in ChatGPT so the output is more accurate and context-rich. That way, you are not creating generic AI content. You are adding business-specific context that helps the page persuade visitors.

4. Optimize existing content

To improve an existing page, use the content optimizer and import the page URL, keyword phrase, and content.

Then run the optimizer and look for the gaps. The goal is not just to write more words. The goal is to make the page more relevant.

A page can be 1,800 words long and still perform poorly if it is not relevant enough. Relevance matters more than length.

You can revise the copy, add missing topics, and improve the page until the relevance score increases. In the example, the score moved from about 21 to 36, and then to 60 after further revisions.

At that point, the page is often strong enough to launch and test.

5. Create schema markup

ChatGPT can create schema markup, but the first version is often too templated and needs work.

One useful approach is to ask it to add real review data from sites like G2 and Capterra. That makes the schema richer and more detailed.

You can then paste the schema into your site builder or code editor and update the page. This helps traditional search engines understand the page better.

6. Create data-driven content

For data-driven content, start by using the thinking model with extended thinking.

Ask ChatGPT to research a topic and share the findings. The advantage is that it can analyze many sources quickly and synthesize the information for you.

Then take those findings and turn them into an article. To make it stronger, layer in Rankability so the article is also highly relevant.

For example, you can ask it to write about whether dwell time is a Google ranking factor, mention the source, and cover the critical related topics.

Once the draft is ready, you can refine the title and improve the structure. The main heavy lifting is already done.

7. Generate linkbait ideas

You can use a custom prompt syntax to generate linkbait ideas for a business and location.

The reason this works is because the ideas are built around proven linkable frameworks, often based on emotion or a content format that attracts links.

This gives you a fast way to generate strong ideas, then expand on the ones that look most promising.

8. Create free tools

You can use ChatGPT to research the structure and guidelines for a tool, then have it build the tool itself.

In the example, it generated the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in about a minute.

That means you can quickly build simple interactive tools, test them, improve them, and deploy them on your site.

9. Turn it into a link builder

You can use agents to do link prospecting for you instead of manually searching Google.

With the Pro mode agent, you can make up to 40 agent requests a month, which means a lot of prospecting campaigns.

You can ask it to find listicles, prospects, and even contact information, saving a huge amount of time.

10. Track ChatGPT SEO performance

To measure performance, create synthetic prompts based on a seed, such as “plumber in Chesterfield, Missouri.”

Then use a dedicated testing account to run those prompts inside ChatGPT.

Check two things:

- Is your brand mentioned?
- What position does your brand appear in?

That gives you a benchmark for visibility. Then repeat the process across many prompts with different intent so you can measure overall visibility and improve it over time.

That’s a quick look at what’s possible with ChatGPT SEO systems.
