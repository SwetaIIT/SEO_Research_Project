Title: How I'd Use ChatGPT for SEO in 2026

Source: https://youtu.be/zb5oTzHd9l4?si=_TdcY8YtRsQDoiwy

I’m going to show you 10 proven ways to use ChatGPT for SEO without spamming or doing anything risky, and these techniques will save you a massive amount of time and effort in your SEO campaigns. My name is Nathan Gotch and I’ve led hundreds of successful SEO campaigns over the last decade and I’m the CEO of Rankability, a top AI SEO software company. Let’s start with number one, which is filtering through a huge list of keywords based on relevance. Start by going into a keyword research tool like Rankability, download your keyword list, and then copy it into ChatGPT. The goal is to take a huge list of maybe 400 keywords and narrow it down into the most relevant keywords to pursue. This is stage one of filtering keywords but it’s the most important because we want to understand how close a keyword is to making us money. The shorter that gap, the better. The longer the gap, the less valuable the keyword is. For example, if you are a bankruptcy lawyer in Atlanta, you paste your keywords and let ChatGPT assign relevance scores so you can prioritize what actually matters.
ChatGPT SEO technique number two is to group your keywords into clusters. Once you have relevance scores, you take only the best keywords and group them into buckets because SEO works best when you focus on one topic cluster at a time. You don’t want to spread yourself across unrelated topics. Instead, you go deep into one cluster, build authority, and then move on to the next. These clusters also interlink with each other to build topical authority across your website. After that, you expand these same clusters into YouTube, LinkedIn, and other content platforms to strengthen authority further.
ChatGPT SEO technique number three is creating new content that ranks. You start by crawling a website and extracting unique selling propositions and testimonials, then turn that into a structured prompt for AI content generation. This is especially useful for commercial pages. The key is to avoid generic AI content and instead add real business context so the page becomes persuasive and unique. Using the thinking model in ChatGPT improves accuracy because it retrieves and analyzes real website information before generating content. This ensures the output is not generic but tailored to the actual business, improving conversion potential.
ChatGPT SEO technique number four is optimizing existing content. You take an existing URL, enter it into a content optimizer tool, and analyze gaps in relevance and topic coverage. Then you feed that content into ChatGPT and ask it to improve and expand it based on missing topics. You iterate this process until the relevance score improves significantly. For example, a page may go from a low relevance score like 21 to 60 after optimization. The goal is not word count but relevance, because relevance is what drives rankings.
ChatGPT SEO technique number five is generating schema markup. ChatGPT can create schema automatically, but it often needs refinement. You can improve it by adding structured data like reviews from platforms such as G2 or Capterra. Once refined, you implement the schema on your site using tools like Replit or directly into your website builder. Schema helps search engines better understand your page, even though its impact on AI search systems is still evolving.
ChatGPT SEO technique number six is creating data-driven content. You use the thinking model with extended reasoning to research a topic deeply and analyze multiple sources quickly. Instead of manually opening hundreds of tabs, ChatGPT synthesizes information in minutes. You then combine this research with SEO optimization tools to create highly relevant and structured content. This approach ensures your article is both data-backed and optimized for search engines. You typically let the AI decide depth rather than forcing strict word counts, then refine the output afterward for readability and structure.
ChatGPT SEO technique number seven is generating link bait ideas. You use a structured prompt system to generate proven linkable content ideas based on emotional triggers or successful content frameworks. This allows you to quickly build multiple ideas that can be expanded into full campaigns. These ideas are designed specifically to attract backlinks and can be scaled into deeper content strategies.
ChatGPT SEO technique number eight is creating free tools. You can prompt ChatGPT to research and build functional tools based on a required structure or industry standard. It can generate full HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in minutes. You then test, refine, and deploy these tools on your website. This is extremely powerful for link building because free tools naturally attract backlinks and traffic.
ChatGPT SEO technique number nine is turning ChatGPT into a full-time link builder. Instead of manually searching for link prospects, you use AI agents to find opportunities automatically. These agents can identify listicles, gather contact information, and even find emails for outreach. With enough requests per month, you can scale link prospecting across entire industries. This removes the need for manual Google searches and significantly speeds up outreach workflows.
ChatGPT SEO technique number ten is tracking ChatGPT SEO performance. You create synthetic prompts based on real search intent and run them through a dedicated testing account. The goal is to check whether your brand appears in ChatGPT responses and at what position it is mentioned. You measure visibility using a binary system where either your brand appears or it does not, and then track position across multiple prompts. By analyzing many prompts, you get a clear understanding of your overall visibility in AI-generated search responses and can optimize accordingly.
That is just a taste of what is possible. If you want more ChatGPT SEO systems, you continue refining these workflows and apply them across keyword research, content creation, optimization, and brand visibility tracking in AI search systems.
