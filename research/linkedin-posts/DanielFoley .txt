Key things to note for GEO:

1. Searchers using LLMS are more likely to search 5-10+ words on average, this significantly increases the volume of "ZSV" prompts that would otherwise never get tracked

2. Whilst SEOs have Google Search Console for query impression data, AI platforms do not provide keyword data or any impression data - therefore all prompt search volumes are assumed and likely derived from "other" sources as a benchmark

3. Citations are not consistent you have too many variables:

> LLM
> LLM Model
> Personalisation and memory
> Different pipeline/RAG
> Different retrieval sourcing
> Localisation
> Randomisation (natural in LLM output)

4. LLM Models sometimes change whether or not:

> They cite the origin source or the target brand
> Whether any link is given with the citation

I could go on.

I AM PRO doing what you can to be cited and appear more in LLMS.

But I think it's time we had a sensible conversation about the practicalities of:

1. Tracking and the value it brings
2. How you measure impact
3. How you define success and correlation with visibility improvements
4. What GEO actually is that isn't already SEO or something else
